Two issues, OLD and NEW

What the shop is told to make - quantity, profile, material, cut length and weight - read off the single part drawings of two issues and compared part by part. One sheet per value, and one word against each part: increased, decreased, changed, or no change.

This is not a chain and keeps no history: two folders in, one report out. Name the folders so one contains OLD and the other NEW — that is what decides which way round they are read, not the order they are chosen in. Reading them backwards would turn every increase into a decrease.
